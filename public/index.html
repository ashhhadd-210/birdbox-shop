This season’s boxes

100% sustainable timber

Every plank that comes through the door is FSC certified or reclaimed — usually old scaffold boards and joinery offcuts from sites around Kent. Nothing is treated with preservative, because a nesting bird spends six weeks pressed against the inside of that wood.

Boxes, built to last

Twenty winters, not two

A cheap box fails in the same three places: the roof splits, the fixings rust, and the floor rots because water cannot get out. So we overhang every roof by 40mm, use stainless steel throughout, and drill drainage into every floor before it is fitted.

  • Rebated joints, screwed and glued — never stapled
  • Roofs overhang the entrance so rain never reaches the nest
  • Stainless fixings and hanging plates as standard
  • Hinged or lift-off panels, because a box needs cleaning each autumn

Autumn & winter

September is shelter season

Nesting is over, so this is the month to clean out last spring’s boxes and put up anything new — birds prospect nest sites through the winter, and a box that has weathered a few months is far likelier to be taken in March. It is also the last window to give a hedgehog somewhere to hibernate before the first frosts.

  • Clean old boxes with hot water only, never disinfectant
  • Hang new boxes now so they weather before spring
  • Face the entrance between north and east, away from afternoon sun
  • Hedgehog houses want a quiet corner, back to a hedge, by mid-October

About us

Four people and a bandsaw

Hollow & Home started in 2009 in a single garage in Aylesford, making tit boxes for a village fete. The order book got out of hand. Sixteen years later we are still in Kent, still cutting every panel here, and still refusing to sell a box we would not put up in our own gardens.

Why the specifications matter

A nest box is not decorative joinery. Hole diameter decides which species can use it and which predators cannot. Internal depth decides whether a cat can reach the brood. Roof overhang decides whether a week of driving rain drowns it. Every dimension on this site follows published RSPB, Kent Wildlife Trust or Barn Owl Trust guidance, and where those bodies disagree we build to the more conservative figure.

The timber

Two sources only: FSC certified stock from a merchant in Maidstone, and reclaimed boards — old scaffold planks, joinery offcuts, the occasional church pew. Reclaimed timber is denser and better seasoned than anything new, and it costs a tree nothing. Nothing is treated. If a box needs a finish we char it, which is a Japanese technique older than preservative and lasts longer.

The guarantee

Five years on every box, and in practice we have replaced eleven boxes in sixteen years. If a roof splits or a joint opens, send us a photograph and we will make you another.

Trade and stockists

We supply around forty garden centres, wildlife trusts and estate shops. Trade terms start at twelve units mixed. Schools and scout groups get the DIY kit at cost plus postage — just ask.
